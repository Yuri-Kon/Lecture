Blueberries After Dark

November 14, 2025 · 1 min · 117 words

published in: The Shadow of Sirius, 2008

So this is the way the night tastes
one at a time
not early or late

my mother told me
that I was not afraid of the dark
and when I looked it was true

how did she know
so long ago

with her father dead
almost before she could remember
and her mother following him
not long after
and then her grandmother
who had brought her up
and a little later
her only brother
and then her firstborn
gone as soon
as he was born
she knew

译文:(曾虹 译 《天狼星阴影》 人民文学出版社 2017年)

那么这就是夜的滋味了
一颗一颗
不早不晚

我的母亲告诉我
我是不惧黑暗的人
回望时我信了

可她如何知晓
在遥远的从前

当他父亲在她
几乎尚未记事时去世
随后她母亲
再稍后离世的是抚养 她长大的祖母
接着是她唯一的哥哥
和她的头生子
堕地便逝去
那时她就知道
